# FMCG Sales Analytics Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## Project Overview
An interactive FMCG Sales Analytics Dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, product revenue, customer behavior, regional performance, and payment trends.

The dashboard provides business insights through KPI cards, interactive filters, and multiple visualizations to support data-driven decision-making.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Quantity Sold | 53,759 |
| Average Discount | 8% |
| Net Sales | 1,891,331 |
| Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) | 1,121,083 |
| Total Profit | 770,248 |

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Product-Wise Sales
- Analyzes revenue generated by individual products.
- Tomato Ketchup 500g is the highest revenue-generating product.
- Compares product-level sales performance.

### 2. Category-Wise Sales
Revenue contribution by category:

| Category | Sales Contribution |
|---|---:|
| Sauces | 35% |
| Snacks | 30% |
| Bakery | 18% |
| Beverages | 12% |
| Ready-to-Eat | 5% |

### 3. Region-Wise Sales
- North: 40%
- Central: 30%
- West: 30%

### 4. City-Wise Sales
Analyzes sales performance across:
- Delhi
- Noida
- Meerut
- Bahadurgarh
- Faridabad

### 5. Customer-Wise Sales
Tracks sales performance across retail clients, distributors, and stores.

### 6. Payment Mode Analysis

| Payment Mode | Contribution |
|---|---:|
| Cash | 27% |
| Credit | 27% |
| Bank Transfer | 24% |
| UPI | 22% |

## Dashboard Filters
- Invoice Date Filter
- Date range: September 23–30
- Selected date: September 27

## Business Insights
- Sauces contribute the largest share of category revenue.
- North is the leading sales region.
- Tomato Ketchup 500g generates the highest product revenue.
- Cash and credit are the most frequently used payment modes.
- Profit is calculated as Net Sales minus COGS.
